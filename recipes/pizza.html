Recipe for Pizza

Home

Description:

To make a delicious homemade pizza from scratch, prepare a simple yeast dough, let it rise, add your favorite sauce and toppings, and bake it in a very hot oven.

Ingredients:

  • water
  • yeast
  • sugar
  • flour
  • salt
  • tomato sauce
  • oregano
  • mozzarella cheese
  • desired toppings

Steps:

  1. Make Pizza dough by mixing flour, water, yeast, sugar and salt and cover it until it expands.
  2. Create a fresh tomato sauce using canned tomatoes , salt and oregano, shred mozzarella
  3. Cut the desired topping like sausages
  4. preheat the oven to a high temperature(400- 450 F)
  5. Punch down the dough an gently stretch it into circular crust on a floured surface
  6. Transfer the dough into a baking sheet, spread sauce and cheese and toppings
  7. Bake until the crust turns golden and Cheese bubbles
  8. Take out of the oven and cut it using suitable tool
