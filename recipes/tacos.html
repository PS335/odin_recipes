Recipe for Tacos

Home

Description:

Make a quick and easy batch of classic ground beef tacos in just 15 minutes with simple pantry spices and fresh toppings.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • finely diced onions
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 tablespoons of taco seasonings
  • 1 tablespoon tomato past
  • 1/2 cup of water
  • 8 taco shells/ small tortillas
  • Toppings: lettuce, diced tomatoes, cheddar cheese
  • sour cream

Steps:

  1. Cook the beef and aromatics: Brown the ground beef in a skillet for 4 minutes, add diced onions and garlic, and drain excess grease.
  2. Season and simmer: Stir in taco seasoning, tomato paste, and water, then simmer for a few minutes until juicy.
  3. Assemble: Warm the shells or tortillas and fill with the seasoned beef and your favorite toppings.
