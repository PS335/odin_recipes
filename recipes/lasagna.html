Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description:

You can make a classic, delicious homemade lasagna by preparing a rich meat sauce, mixing a creamy cheese blend, and layering everything before baking.

Ingredients:

  • ground beef
  • sauce
  • onion, gsrlic
  • pasta sauce
  • ricotta cheese, mozarella cheese
  • 2 large eggs
  • oregano
  • salt and black paper
  • lasagna noodles 8-10

Steps:

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). If using standard noodles, boil them in a large pot of salted water until al dente, then drain
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet or pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and cook until soft. Add the ground meat and cook until completely browned.
  3. Stir in the garlic and seasonings during the last minute, then drain any excess grease. Pour in your marinara sauce and let it simmer for 10 minutes to thicken.
  4. In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta (or cottage cheese), half of the mozzarella, grated Parmesan, eggs, salt, and pepper. Stir until well blended.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a greased 9x13-inch baking dish so the noodles do not stick. Arrange a layer of lasagna noodles over the sauce. Spread a third of the cheese mixture over the noodles, then top with a generous layer of meat sauce. Repeat this layering process two more times.
  6. Finish with a final layer of noodles, the remaining meat sauce, and the rest of the mozzarella cheese on top. Spray a sheet of aluminum foil with non-stick cooking spray and cover the baking dish (spray-side down)
  7. Bake covered at 375°F for 30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15 minutes until the top is golden and bubbling.
  8. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing so the layers hold their shape.
